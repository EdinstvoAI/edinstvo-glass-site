Merging is opt-in, per element. Pieces with merge become one shape when they get close. Everything else stays a body of its own.

Drag the two on the left apart and back. The one on the right has no merge, so it slides over them.

How far it reaches

The number is the reach of the melt in pixels, from 0 to 64. merge on the group sets it for every child, and merge="0" on a child opts out.

Merged pieces use one material, configured on the group. Set frost on the group to control their shared blur. Unmerged children can override their own material. Only pieces inside the same group can merge, up to 64 of them.
