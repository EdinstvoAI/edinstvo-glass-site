Text and other HTML behind glass don't bend by default. Here is why, and how to get it anyway.

Why

A page can't read its own pixels, so glass can only use sources it can load itself. A paragraph isn't one of them. See How glass works.

Draw the text on a canvas

A canvas is something glass can read. Draw your text there and put glass over it.

await document.fonts.load("700 120px Inter");

const context = canvas.getContext("2d");
context.font = "700 120px Inter";
context.fillStyle = "#e54d33";
context.fillText("glass", 40, 160);

glass.invalidate();

Three details

Wait for the font, or the canvas draws a fallback. Multiply the canvas size by devicePixelRatio, or the text looks soft on sharp screens. Call invalidate() after drawing, so glass knows the picture changed.

Keep it accessible

Text on a canvas isn't real text. Keep a real heading on the page for screen readers and search.
