What it can't do yet, so you find out here and not in production.

Text behind the glass

Text and regular DOM behind the glass aren't refracted, only images, video, canvas, solid colours and other glass. If you need them, draw them to a canvas and pass it as the source.

CSS gradients

They aren't read yet. Glass skips them and looks further down the stack.

Cross-origin images

They need CORS headers. Without them that element falls back to CSS.

Stacking contexts

layer compares numbers across the whole page and doesn't look at nested stacking contexts.
