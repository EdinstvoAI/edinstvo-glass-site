Real glass for the web. One WebGL material that bends light the same way on macOS and on Windows, wrapped in a single tag that works in any framework.

Drag it. Press it. That is the whole library in one rectangle.

Why not backdrop-filter

backdrop-filter blurs. It can't bend anything, so the edge of the element stays a flat cut. Glass here is a lens: light goes through a curved rim by Snell's law, colours split where the surface bends, and the middle stays calm.

What you get

  • Optics. Refraction, colour split, true gaussian frost, light that knows its angle.
  • Motion. Springs on size and position, jelly under a drag, light from under the finger.
  • Merge. Pieces melt into one shape when they get close.
  • Layers. Glass sees the glass under it.

How it stays fast

One WebGL context for the whole page. A frame is drawn only when something changes, so a still page costs nothing. Frost is computed once at a small size and reused. There are no dependencies, and the package is about 26 kB.
